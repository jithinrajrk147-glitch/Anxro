§ 02

About Anxro

Anxro is an all-in-one learning and productivity Progressive Web App (PWA) developed by Black Strings, an independent software development entity based in India, designed for students, learners, and productivity enthusiasts across India and beyond.

  • Platform Type: Progressive Web App (PWA) — installable on any device, works offline
  • Primary Market: India — content available in Hindi and English
  • Interface: Mobile-first, dark-themed, bottom navigation PWA
  • Developed by: Black Strings (independent developer)
  • Official URL: jithinrajrk147-glitch.github.io/Anxro
  • Business Website: blackstring-offical.github.io/Black-string-/
  • Target Audience: Students, self-learners, traders, and productivity users

Anxro is free to use and does not require account creation or registration to access core features. All data is stored locally on your device.
